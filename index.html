Spatial read

Airport and rental car start/end near Boston Logan by default.
Hyannis is the ferry base. Mashpee is close enough for a clean Saturday ferry day.
Mashpee is southwest of Hyannis. Exact venue address still needs confirmation.
Nantucket is offshore. Plan it as passenger ferry plus walking/shuttle, not rental car.

Recommendation

Default plan
Use Saturday for Nantucket and Sunday for the scenic return. Keep the island day walkable, eat well near the wharf, then decide Sunday morning whether to do the easy Sandwich/Woods Hole route or the full Outer Cape run to Provincetown and Race Point.

On the map, the named labels are the actual route stops. The route cards below show the drive time for each leg and link directly into Google Maps.

Flight frame

ATL -> BOS Thursday morning; BOS -> ATL Monday 6:50-9:33am.

Sunday route choice

Balanced Sunday

  • South Cape Beach or The Knob in the morning.
  • Sandwich Boardwalk, Fishermen's View, or Tree House on the way back.
  • Clean arrival in Boston before dinner.

Provincetown / Race Point

  • Coolest scenery: dunes, Race Point, Commercial Street.
  • Best as a morning-start route with lunch or drinks in Provincetown.
  • For a shorter dunes hit, use Wellfleet / Marconi and turn back toward Boston.
